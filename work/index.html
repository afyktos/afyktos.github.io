Project 01

Cultural event identity

Kavala: The Timeless
Apple of Discord

Organisation
20th Armoured Division
Location
Kavala, Greece
Deliverables
Poster, invitation, roll-up, commemorative programme

A unified visual identity for a public historical event, translated from a single concept into formats ranging from a formal invitation to a three-metre outdoor installation.

The creative direction connects Kavala’s coastal character with the symbolic golden apple, using archival texture, classical ornament and a restrained blue-and-gold palette. The system was adapted for both close reading and high-visibility public display.

Process note AI-assisted imagery was developed and art-directed in response to supplied visual requests. Concept development, composition, typography, layout, format adaptation and final artwork preparation were completed by Afyktos.

Project 02

Internal event campaign

Sports Day

Organisation
20th Armoured Division
Deliverables
Two poster directions, event invitation

Two distinct creative routes for an internal wellness event, each translated into clear, approachable print communication.

The brief called for an inviting tone outside the visual language of formal military communication. Botanical forms, warm colour and active figures create an accessible identity while retaining the organisation mark and essential event information.

Service context

Nine months.
More than one role.

These projects were produced during military service at the IT Research Office of the 20th Armoured Division. Alongside visual communication assignments, the placement included end-user support, ticket handling, systems monitoring and day-to-day technical assistance.
